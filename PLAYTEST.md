# Echo Loom — Playtest Notes

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Tap / click | Cast a light pulse. Short taps aim forward into the chamber. |
| Hold + drag | Preview a curved beam; release to cast along that bend. |
| Mouse | Same as touch for desktop testing. |

On first load of each chamber, a hint appears for ~10 seconds.

## Levels

### Chamber I — First Light (tutorial)
- 3 constellation stars
- 2 simple glass panes
- Easy to awaken stars with direct shots or a single bounce
- Goal: learn casting and gentle bending

### Chamber II — Refraction Hall
- 4 stars
- 4 glass mirrors + 1 crystal
- Needs intentional aim and at least a few reflections
- Stars sit higher and deeper; curve shots help reach awkward angles

### Chamber III — The Silent Vault
- 5 stars
- 5 glass panes + 3 crystals
- Satisfying multi-bounce puzzle chamber
- Clear all stars for the win screen ("The Loom Hums")

## Flow

Title → Begin → Chamber I → clear banner → Chamber II → clear banner → Chamber III → win screen → Weave Again

## Tips

- Drag sideways to wrap light around a pane.
- Crystal faces scatter reflections — useful for side stars.
- You can cast multiple pulses; there is no pause menu in v1.
